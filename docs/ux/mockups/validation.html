Nordea 1, SICAV · Prospectus · June 2026page 123 of 944

Share classes

The SICAV may issue shares of the following classes in each fund. The classes differ in the investors they may be offered to, the minimum initial investment and the fees charged.

P All investors. No minimum initial investment.

I Institutional investors. Minimum initial investment: EUR 75,000 or equivalent in other currencies, waived for funds of the Nordea group.

X Institutional investors that have a specific agreement with the management company.

Shares of class I may only be acquired by institutional investors within the meaning of article 174 of the 2010 Law. The board may refuse an application for class I shares where the applicant cannot demonstrate its status.

Classes are available in the reference currency of the fund and in the currencies shown in the table of each fund.

The prospectus restricts class I to institutional investors under art. 174 of the 2010 Law, which includes professionals investing for retail clients under a discretionary mandate. The BI-EUR KID says the fund suits all investors.
